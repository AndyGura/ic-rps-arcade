PLAY ROCK PAPER SCISSORS ONLINE

RPS Arcade is a free online Rock Paper Scissors game with a retro arcade look: pixel-art fighters, a synthwave grid, 8-bit sound and a CRT filter. Play two-player matches against real people, or take on the CPU. No download and no sign-up: open the page and you get a player ID.

How to play

  1. New game: pick your seat, the mode (Classic or +Well), how many round wins take the match (1 to 9) and whether the table is open or locked with an access code.
  2. Arcade floor: join an open table someone else is waiting at.
  3. VS CPU: play a bot when nobody else is around (shown when bots are available).
  4. Invite a friend: copy the invite link on the waiting screen and send it; it takes them straight to your table.
  5. Both players choose a move in secret. When both are in, the round is revealed: Ready? Fight! K.O.

Rules

  • Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.
  • In +Well mode a fourth move joins: the well beats rock and scissors, and paper beats the well.
  • A tie scores for nobody. The first player to reach the target number of round wins takes the match.
  • The Hi-Scores board ranks players by ELO rating.

Controls

1–4 or R P S W pick a move, Enter asks for a rematch, M turns sound on or off and C toggles the CRT filter. On a phone, just tap.

On the Internet Computer

Tables, moves and results live in a smart contract (a canister) on the Internet Computer, not on a private game server. Players on other frontends of the same game meet you at the same tables.
